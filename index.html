ABOUT GCI

Development begins
with awareness.

Gratitude Consulting Indonesia is a human and organizational development consulting practice focused on connecting people, process, action, and performance.

We believe meaningful development is not simply about adding knowledge or motivation. Sustainable change requires awareness, understanding of the self, intentional action, and continuous feedback.

Gratitude is part of our distinctive approach: not as a solution to every problem, but as a practice that can enable awareness and create space for better choices and actions.
